/ A FIVE-MINUTE CHECK-IN

Career path
reflection.

Use these questions to turn a broad feeling of ambition into a more actionable next move.

  1. What work makes me feel useful or curious?Think about subjects, tasks, community needs and problems you want to understand.
  2. What proof of skill do I already have?Class projects, volunteering, responsibilities and personal learning all count as evidence.
  3. What is one capability I can improve this month?Choose a small, specific skill that helps the route you are considering.
  4. Who could help me see this route more clearly?Ask one thoughtful question of a lecturer, peer, mentor or professional.

/ BEFORE YOU SEND

Application
checklist.

  • ✓ I understand the role or programme and its stated requirements.
  • ✓ My CV or profile is current and easy to read.
  • ✓ My contact details are accurate and professional.
  • ✓ My application answers use examples from my own experience.
  • ✓ I have checked the source and deadline.

/ STAY ALERT

Opportunity
search, safely.

YCH does not currently operate a live opportunity database. When you search independently, use original employer or institution pages where possible and stop if something feels unclear.

  • Do not pay an unknown party to secure a job or internship.
  • Verify the organisation and contact address independently.
  • Read the privacy terms before sharing identity documents.
