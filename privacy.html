This notice describes the current static version of the YCH website. It must be reviewed and updated before the site starts collecting data through a server-side form, newsletter or user account.

What happens now

The current contact form does not transmit form details to a YCH server. When you choose “Prepare email enquiry,” your own email application is opened with a pre-filled message addressed to the public YCH email address. Your email provider’s privacy practices then apply.

This static website does not currently create user accounts, subscribe visitors to a newsletter or store submitted contact enquiries.

Before data collection begins

Before YCH enables a hosted contact form, newsletter, accounts, analytics or any third-party service, it should publish a fuller notice that identifies the data controller, purpose, lawful basis, retention period, vendors, international transfers and the process for exercising privacy rights.

Event photographs

YCH should obtain appropriate consent and permissions before publishing identifiable photographs of participants—especially young people—and should provide clear alt text and captions. Demonstration imagery in this website is labelled as illustrative and must not be presented as YCH activity photography.

Questions

For questions about this notice or contact details, email youthcareerhubtanzania@gmail.com.
