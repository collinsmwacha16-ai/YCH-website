Programmes with
a purpose.

Our programme areas are a framework for the practical, people-centred support that Youth Career Hub is building with and for young people.

/ OUR PROGRAMME AREAS

Each area meets a different part of the career journey. Specific activities, dates and participation details will be published only when confirmed.

⌕

01

Career
development

Direction starts with context.

Career conversations, planning tools and exposure to possible roles help young people make informed decisions about study, work and enterprise.

  • Career exploration
  • Work-readiness guidance
  • Application preparation
02

Digital &
professional skills

Skills that travel with you.

Practical learning that strengthens communication, digital fluency, collaboration and the confidence to operate in professional settings.

  • Workplace communication
  • Digital capability
  • Personal productivity
03

Mentorship &
connection

Progress is a team effort.

Conversations and community spaces that make professional insight more accessible and create a bridge between young people and people with experience to share.

  • Peer learning
  • Mentor engagement
  • Professional networking
04

Enterprise &
innovation

Ideas deserve a route forward.

Encouragement and foundational resources for young people interested in solving problems, building initiatives and exploring entrepreneurship responsibly.

  • Idea development
  • Entrepreneurial mindset
  • Community problem-solving
05

Youth leadership
& participation

Lead with purpose.

Spaces to develop the confidence, collaboration and civic awareness needed to contribute thoughtfully to workplaces and communities.

  • Leadership practice
  • Team collaboration
  • Community engagement
