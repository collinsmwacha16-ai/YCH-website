OUR MISSION

Make the next
step feel possible.

We are working toward a Tanzania where young people can approach their working lives with the skills, context and community to choose a direction with confidence.

YCH→YCH→YCH→YCH

/ THE WHY

The transition from education to work should not depend on who already knows the right person, understands the system, or can afford to make a wrong turn.

YCH is shaped by a simple idea: access to good guidance, practical learning and supportive networks can make a meaningful difference in how a young person sees—and acts on—their future.

/ OUR VISION

A generation of young people who can shape meaningful, resilient futures.

01

/ OUR MISSION

To connect students and graduates with career insight, relevant skills, trusted networks and pathways to opportunity.

02

/ OUR COMMITMENTS

What we will
hold ourselves to.

01 / Clarity

Say what is known.

We will distinguish a confirmed programme, opportunity or result from an idea still in development.

02 / Relevance

Stay close to real needs.

We will design with the changing expectations of students, graduates and the world of work in view.

03 / Agency

Keep young people in the lead.

We will offer tools, context and connection—not promise to choose a future for anyone.

04 / Care

Build trust deliberately.

We will respect personal information, welcome questions and make space for different starting points.

/ OUR METHOD

Discover.
Develop.
Connect.

Our programme design follows a simple rhythm: help young people understand their options, grow skills through practical experiences and form relationships that support progress over time.
